CultureCompass Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

CultureCompass is published by Weegix Technology Solutions LLC ("Weegix"). This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

Information stored on your device

Your settings, language choice, home location choice, daily tip time, saved places, starred phrases, checklist progress and quiz scores are stored only on your device. You can delete them at any time with Settings > Reset progress, or by uninstalling the app.

Notifications

If you turn on the optional daily culture tip, the app asks for permission to show notifications. The tips are scheduled on your device; nothing is sent to or received from Weegix. You can turn them off at any time in the app's Settings or in your phone settings.

Text-to-speech

The listen feature uses the text-to-speech engine already installed on your phone. Weegix does not receive any audio or text from this feature.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the app passes text to your phone's share menu. What happens next is controlled by the app you choose.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date.
